# Human Go-Live Decision v1 — Claim Boundary

This module records an explicit human decision after `READY_FOR_HUMAN_GO_LIVE_DECISION`.

It may record approval, conditional approval, deferral, or rejection. Even when approval is recorded, the module does **not** execute a deployment, certify production security, establish legal approval, establish a certified valuation, or authorize any investment transaction.

Operational deployment remains a separate controlled action with its own execution evidence, rollback controls, monitoring, and human accountability.
