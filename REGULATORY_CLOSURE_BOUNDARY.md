# Regulatory Closure Evidence v1 — Claim Boundary

This module records caller-supplied legal/regulatory classification evidence for human production-readiness review.

It does **not**:

- determine Saudi legal or regulatory status by itself;
- provide legal advice;
- issue or verify a government license independently;
- convert a software decision-support system into licensed professional practice;
- establish legal approval, certified valuation status, brokerage authority, or transaction authority;
- replace review by qualified Saudi legal/regulatory professionals or the competent authority.

## Required evidence

A complete pack requires:

1. an explicit jurisdiction and as-of date;
2. a completed classification review by legal counsel or an authorized regulatory reviewer;
3. an explicit operating classification and licensing/authorization determination;
4. authorization evidence when the reviewer classifies the scope as regulated or authorization-dependent;
5. authoritative source provenance, version/hash, last-verification date, and review-after date;
6. permitted uses, prohibited uses, privacy review, disclosure/terms review, and human-professional boundaries;
7. a complete evidence-reference chain.

`EVIDENCE_PACK_COMPLETE` means only that the supplied evidence package satisfies these deterministic completeness and consistency checks. It remains non-certifying and requires human production-readiness review.
