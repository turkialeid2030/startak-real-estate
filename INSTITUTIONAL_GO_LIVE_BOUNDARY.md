# Institutional Go-Live Gate v1 — Claim Boundary

The gate assembles deterministic evidence for a **human institutional go-live decision**.

`READY_FOR_HUMAN_GO_LIVE_DECISION` means only that the supplied production-readiness, controlled-pilot, market-evidence, external-valuation-validation, regulatory-closure, and review-packet evidence passed the gate's completeness and consistency checks.

It does **not**:

- authorize a production deployment;
- certify production security;
- establish legal or regulatory approval;
- establish a certified valuation;
- create a statistical guarantee of future valuation accuracy;
- authorize a transaction;
- replace professional, security, legal, valuation, or investment-committee judgment.

The gate requires caller-supplied governance policy for required market usages and does not invent market-data or valuation-validation thresholds.
