# P26 Reviewer Non-Claims

Independent review of the canonical re-baseline proposal does not establish or authorize any of the following:

- historical verification of the unavailable canonical original;
- Saudi legal or regulatory compliance;
- professional valuation authority;
- production authentication or persistence validation;
- production security or resilience validation;
- release, merge, deployment or go-live authority;
- transaction execution.

The review is limited to the re-baseline governance question: whether the unavailable legacy file baseline may be superseded by the explicitly scoped repository-native successor baseline after the required governance process.
