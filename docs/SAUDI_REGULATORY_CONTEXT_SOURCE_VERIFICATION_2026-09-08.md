# Saudi Regulatory Context Source Verification — 2026-09-08

## Purpose
Record current primary-source evidence for Saudi professional valuation, real-estate regulation, capital-markets valuation, central-bank supervised valuation, financial-reporting fair value, personal-data protection and real-estate transaction tax contexts.

This packet supports purpose-based routing and evidence governance only. It is not a Saudi legal opinion and does not activate any production rule.

## Verified context groups

### Taqeem professional regulation
Taqeem's current rules catalogue lists the executive regulation of the Accredited Valuers Law, ethics/rules of the valuation profession, rules governing real-estate valuation services for financing entities, and professional-certificate rules. The page was observed with a last-modified date of 3 August 2026.

Official source:
`https://taqeem.gov.sa/rules/category/llyh-ltnfydhy-wlqw-d-4`

Taqeem also announced on 1 April 2026 a model for measuring the quality of real-estate valuation reports, aimed at professional self-review before final approval and addressing completeness, clarity and adherence to professional standards.

Official source:
`https://taqeem.gov.sa/news/details/tqyym-ttlq-nmwdhj-qys-jwd-tqryr-tqyym-l-qrt-1347`

### REGA real-estate contributions context
The executive regulation of the Real Estate Contributions Law contains a context-specific reconciliation rule under which REGA uses the average between accredited valuation reports and excludes/replaces a valuation differing from the other two by more than 10%.

Official source:
`https://rega.gov.sa/الأنظمة-والقرارات/الأنظمة-واللوائح-والأدلة/اللوائح/اللائحة-التنفيذية-لنظام-المساهمات-العقارية/`

STARTAK must not generalize this contribution-specific mechanism into an ordinary valuation-reconciliation formula.

### CMA real-estate investment fund context
The CMA Real Estate Investment Funds Regulations currently available from the official CMA source require qualified accredited valuers in the applicable fund context and prescribe minimum valuation-report content including method/assumptions, real-estate market analysis, property detail and risk. The regulation also includes three-month report-age requirements for fund purchase/sale decisions, two accredited valuation reports in applicable public-offering contexts, and market-rent disclosure where contractual and market rent differ for income-producing property.

Official source:
`https://cma.org.sa/RulesRegulations/Regulations/Documents/Real_Estate_Investment_Funds_Regulations_2025_AR.pdf`

These rules are CMA fund-context controls, not universal rules for every STARTAK assignment.

### SAMA supervised financing/insurance context
The SAMA Rulebook's active instructions for supervised real-estate valuation clients require use of licensed accredited real-estate valuers, governance separation between financing/insurance decisions and valuation, avoidance of improper influence, provision of sufficient information, and no client forcing of specific valuation methods.

Official source:
`https://rulebook.sama.gov.sa/ar/التزامات-عملاء-التقييم-العقاري-الخاضعين-لإشراف-ورقابة-البنك-المركزي`

SAMA's active Article 64 dated 11 January 2026 also requires collateral to be capable of valuation and enforcement, to be valued and legally checked before financing, and to be periodically revalued where materially market-sensitive.

Official source:
`https://rulebook.sama.gov.sa/ar/المادة-الرابعة-والستون-3`

STARTAK may route these as supervised-entity context requirements, but it must not issue a legal-soundness or credit-approval conclusion.

### SOCPA / IFRS 13 financial-reporting context
SOCPA's endorsed Arabic IFRS 13 material states that SOCPA adopted IFRS 13 Fair Value Measurement as issued, without modification.

Official source:
`https://socpa.org.sa/getattachment/Socpa/Professional-standards/Accounting-standards/Endorsed/12-IFRS-13-2025.pdf.aspx?lang=ar-SA`

This belongs only to financial-reporting fair-value routing; it is not a substitute for investment analysis and does not establish auditor approval.

### SDAIA / PDPL context
The National Data Governance Platform publishes the Personal Data Protection Law and identifies the Saudi Data & AI Authority as the competent authority supervising implementation. The PDPL includes conditions on cross-border transfers, including appropriate protection and minimum-necessary-data principles, subject to the system and regulations.

Official source:
`https://dgp.sdaia.gov.sa/wps/portal/pdp/knowledgecenter/details/PDPL`

A dedicated cross-border transfer regulation also exists and defines appropriate safeguards for relevant transfer scenarios.

Official source:
`https://dgp.sdaia.gov.sa/wps/portal/pdp/knowledgecenter/details/RegulationonPersonalDataTransferOutsidetheKingdom`

Source verification does not establish STARTAK's lawful basis, controller/processor role, retention compliance, localization position, transfer mechanism or subprocessor compliance for a particular deployment.

### ZATCA / RETT context
ZATCA's current Real Estate Transaction Tax page states a 5% RETT rate, identifies the current law under Royal Decree M/84 dated 19/03/1446H, and states that it entered into force on 10 April 2025. The page was last updated 31 August 2026.

Official source:
`https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Pages/RETTRegulation.aspx`

The current implementing regulations were approved by ZATCA Board Decision 01-03-25 dated 24 March 2025.

Official source:
`https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Pages/New-RETT.aspx`

No statutory RETT output can be derived from a property valuation alone; transaction facts, exceptions and competent tax review remain required.
