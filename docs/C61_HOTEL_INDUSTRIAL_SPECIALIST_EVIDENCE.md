# C61 — Operational and interest controls, hotel and industrial

## Existing components deliberately preserved
- Wave 14B historical hotel operating packet: room nights, occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, GOP, operator fees and FF&E reserve. Its resulting operating surplus is **not** real-property NOI, external audit or valuation.
- Wave 14G specialized interest separation: keeps hotel land/building/real estate apart from FF&E, management agreement, intangible/franchise value and enterprise operating profits.
- Industrial-logistics asset adapter: lease structure, permitted building status, net leasable area, truck loading doors, electric service, warehouse clear height and permitted status metadata.

## C61 additional engineering
`src/specialized-assets/specialist-operational-gate.js` takes canonical verified Wave14B and Wave14G record hashes, and a separately documented hotel income-reconciliation bridge. The bridge must reconcile historical after-FF&E operating surplus, an explicitly supplied business/intangible exclusion, and reviewed property-specific normalization to the proposed property NOI; it is never silently generated by room revenue or RevPAR.

For industrial subtype, the gate rejects missing full internal inspection, unverified building permits, unavailable built/lettable area, loading clearance, power capacity, floor loading, permitted-use record and unknown operating expense/lease obligation. Environmental and soil risk, fire safety, title/rights, zoning, site inspections, deferred capex and third-party review all require traceable reviewed evidence packs.

## Limits
Every source right, independently audited hotel statement, industrial permit, environmental finding, human valuation/license and actual Saudi comparables still needs an independent legally authorized reviewer. The C61 structural gate returns **READY_FOR_EXTERNAL_SPECIALIST_REVIEW**, not actual approval, licensed appraisal or report export permission. It is not automatically wired into all existing route handlers. That is C62 issue #628 and must not be represented as complete by existence of this module.
