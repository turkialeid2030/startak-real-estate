# Official Valuation Standards Source Verification — 2026-09-08

## Purpose
Record primary-source evidence for the current IVS and RICS valuation-standard editions/effective dates and Saudi/Taqeem adoption context without changing any production Standards Registry record to `ACTIVE`.

This is an evidence phase only. It is not a legal opinion, professional applicability determination, or standards-conformance certification.

## Primary-source findings

### International Valuation Standards (IVS)
Official IVSC material states that the latest IVS edition was published on 31 January 2024 and is effective for valuations performed on or after 31 January 2025. IVSC permits early adoption and requires historical/retrospective work to document the edition relied upon and applicable at the valuation date.

Official source:
`https://ivsc.org/new-edition-of-the-international-valuation-standards-ivs-published/`

IVSC's Middle East & Africa page describes Taqeem as the statutory Saudi valuation regulator, states that Taqeem has adopted IVS as the foundation of its licensing/accreditation framework, and states that the latest IVS effective 31 January 2025 is adopted in the Kingdom.

Official source:
`https://ivsc.org/ivsc-mea/`

Taqeem also hosts an Arabic IVS Basis for Conclusions document expressly referring to IVS effective from 31 January 2025.

Official Saudi source:
`https://www.taqeem.gov.sa/web/content/portal.library/118/attachment_ar?download=false`

### RICS Red Book Global Standards
RICS' official current Red Book page identifies the current edition as the 2025 Global Standards and confirms an effective date of 31 January 2025, aligned with the IVS effective from the same date.

Official source:
`https://www.rics.org/profession-standards/rics-standards-and-guidance/sector-standards/valuation-standards/red-book/red-book-global`

Taqeem hosts the Arabic RICS Global Valuation Standards publication dated December 2024 and effective 31 January 2025. The hosted text states that national legal requirements take priority where they conflict with the RICS requirements.

Official Saudi source:
`https://taqeem.gov.sa/web/content/portal.library/244/attachment_ar?download=false`

## Important source-catalog discrepancy
The Taqeem digital library, last observed with a page-modified date of 29 July 2026, visibly lists a RICS 2025 item but still labels the directly listed International Valuation Standards download as `معايير التقييم الدولية 2022`. At the same time, other Taqeem-hosted and IVSC primary sources reference IVS effective 31 January 2025.

Official Saudi library:
`https://www.taqeem.gov.sa/digital-lib`

This must be treated as a version/catalog-label ambiguity. STARTAK must not silently assume that the item labelled IVS 2022 is the canonical 2025 production text. The specific Taqeem-hosted canonical IVS text/version should be confirmed before activation.
