# STARTAK Real Estate — Official Saudi Regulatory Source Verification — Phase 1

Date: 2026-09-08

## Purpose

This evidence package records primary Saudi regulatory/accounting/tax/data-protection sources relevant to STARTAK Real Estate. It is source verification only.

It does **not** establish a legal opinion, licensing conclusion, professional valuation authority, standards activation, production security/compliance, merge authority, deployment authority or transaction authority.

Operating mode remains `UNLICENSED_DECISION_SUPPORT`.

## Verified official-source families

### REGA — Real Estate Brokerage Law and Implementing Regulations

Official REGA sources verify that the Real Estate Brokerage Law and its implementing regulations are active and regulate covered real-estate brokerage and real-estate services. The law includes real-estate analysis and consultancy within the statutory definition of real-estate services and requires licensing for covered activities.

This evidence does **not** conclude that STARTAK itself requires a REGA license. That determination depends on the actual service offered, customer workflow, contractual role, commercial activity and Saudi legal characterization.

### CMA — Real Estate Investment Funds Regulations 2025

The official CMA real-estate investment-funds regulation contains purpose-specific valuation requirements. Article 36 includes, among other controls, two accredited valuers for purchase/sale decisions, independence, Taqeem fellowship, minimum valuation-report content and a three-month report-age limitation for purchase/sale reliance.

These requirements are not global defaults for every STARTAK case. They must be routed only when the assignment falls within the relevant CMA fund context.

### ZATCA — Real Estate Transaction Tax

The official ZATCA source states that the current Real Estate Transaction Tax law imposes a 5% tax and that the current law came into effect on 10 April 2025. ZATCA also maintains the official implementing-rules page.

STARTAK must not hard-code a universal 5% payable amount without transaction-specific routing for exemptions, liability, timing and other conditions.

### SOCPA — Saudi endorsement of international standards

SOCPA publishes official endorsement documents for international accounting and auditing standards in Saudi Arabia. The evidence also demonstrates effective-date governance: the IFRS for SMEs 2025 edition is identified as replacing the 2015 edition for annual periods beginning on or after 1 January 2027, with early application permitted.

This supports a strict `ACTIVE` / `FUTURE` lifecycle model and prevents assuming that unmodified global IFRS text applies automatically in Saudi Arabia.

### SDAIA — Personal Data Protection Law

SDAIA's National Data Governance Platform publishes the Personal Data Protection Law and its executive regulations. Source verification does not equal PDPL compliance.

A production compliance conclusion still requires, at minimum, system/data-flow inventory, controller/processor characterization, lawful-basis analysis, retention, data-subject rights, transfer controls, processor arrangements, incident handling and legal/data-governance review.

### SAMA — Real Estate Finance Law

The SAMA Rulebook confirms that SAMA regulates the real-estate finance sector and may issue real-estate finance standards/procedures and finance-cost disclosure principles.

SAMA rules are therefore context-specific inputs for financing workflows, not a universal valuation methodology source.
