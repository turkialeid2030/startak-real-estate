# Documented operating cycle: receivables, planned CAPEX, successor leases

This delivery adds three concrete operating workflows to the existing case workspace. It does not close the full institutional or commercial audit.

## Rent document register

The optional `receivablesLedger` records CHARGE, RECEIPT, ALLOCATION and REVERSAL events. Amounts are positive safe integer halalas. The UI accepts SAR with at most two decimals and requires source and distinct local-review references, with confirmation invalidated after edits. Empty/missing amounts cannot become zero. No events means unavailable totals, not confirmed zero business activity.

Charges belong to an existing lease and cover an exclusive-end rent period inside that lease. Active charge periods cannot overlap for the same lease. The register supports net rent only, not taxes, deposits, fees, split invoices over the same rent period, tax invoice generation or bank integration. Each entry ID and each document/transaction reference within its type are unique, including after reversal. Receipts can remain unallocated. An allocation must reference an earlier active charge and receipt for the same lease, may not exceed either balance, and may not predate them. A charge or receipt with active allocations cannot be reversed until its allocations are reversed. Original events remain in the export. Reversal is correction of an entry, not a refund or write-off.

The summary reports gross recorded charges, actual entered receipts, allocations, unpaid charges, unapplied receipts, receipt months and due-date aging. Not due, due today and overdue balances are distinct. There is no implicit cross-lease offset. Coverage is only the entered documents; no independent proof of completeness or bank authenticity is claimed. Legacy period collections are preserved separately and are never added to the ledger or automatically rewritten into NOI. A 5000-event limit bounds replay; totals fail on unsafe integer overflow. Import replays validation; existing lease/schedule edits preserve the ledger. Stale drafts and repeated in-flight submissions are blocked.

## Explicit capital payment dates

The optional `capitalPaymentPlan` distributes the estimated cost of every supplied CAPEX item across explicit dates. Source/review references and confirmation are required. Plans reconcile in integer halalas to each adopted, sourced cost; missing cost, incorrect currency, unadopted cost, mismatched totals or past planned dates withhold net after CAPEX. No maintenance deadline is silently used as a payment date. Payments outside the twelve-month horizon are disclosed separately. An empty reviewed plan declares zero for the supplied inventory only; it does not prove that no works are missing. Plans can use up to 2000 item/date entries. Cost changes invalidate the projection until the plan reconciles again.

The run-off retains net before CAPEX and separately presents scheduled CAPEX and analytical net after it. Rent is still accrual and OPEX is still normalized allocation; this is not a cash balance or collection forecast. No spending authorization, main-engine mutation, tax, financing or completion of the physical works is implied. Existing item inventory is imported with the operating case; this editor schedules those items rather than performing a technical survey or creating qualified item estimates.

## Documented successor leases

A successor lease can be recorded against an existing unit, with a new unique lease ID, an existing tenant, future dates, explicit periodic rent and source/adoption references. It cannot precede the prior lease expiry or overlap another active/future lease for the unit. The old lease stays intact. The new lease starts with fixed rent in the prior frequency; security and escalation are not copied. A dated rent schedule can be added explicitly afterward. Current occupancy metrics are unchanged, and any gap remains without contractual income. This is a document-entry workflow, not assumed renewal, predicted market rent, regulatory permission, tenant authentication or automatic lifecycle rollover.

## Documented completed refunds

REFUND entries now record an already completed return against an active original receipt, with a unique transaction reference, reason, date, source and local review. Refunds consume only the receipt's unallocated amount; allocated cash must first be released through a documented allocation correction, which reopens the charge balance. Gross receipts, refunds, net receipts and available unallocated cash are separate. Refund months and linked lease IDs are exported; the original charge is never cancelled by a refund. Refund corrections restore the available balance but do not recover money from a bank. A receipt with active refunds cannot be reversed. References remain reserved after correction. Refund-related movements must follow the latest recorded movement date for that receipt, including corrections; legacy ledgers without refunds remain compatible. Import replays the same rules.

This is documentation of completed rent-only refunds, not payment execution, tax credit notes, bank reconciliation or proof of an authorized transfer. Focused tests cover balance conservation, full/partial returns, over-refund, over-allocation after refund, chronology, duplicate references, correction ordering and snapshot replay. Browser acceptance includes rejected over-refund, confirmation invalidation, successful refund, blocked receipt reversal, correction and persistence.

## Documented rent charge reductions (13 October)

CREDIT entries record a sourced reduction against an active original charge, with a unique document reference, reason and local review. Reductions cannot exceed unpaid charges: existing allocations must first be explicitly corrected if the underlying document reduces a paid amount. Gross charges, reductions, net charges, allocations and outstanding balances remain separate. Aging uses the resulting outstanding balance, retaining the original due date. A reduction does not change cash receipts, execute a refund, or alter contractual rent/NOI. Refunded cash and reductions are independent events with independent evidence.

The original charge cannot be reversed while a reduction remains active. Correcting a reduction restores the receivable, keeps its original reference reserved and requires date order relative to other recorded movements of that charge. Import replays all rules. This local record is not a tax credit note, a legal waiver, proof of reviewer identity or authority to forgive debt. Tests cover partial/full reduction, paid amounts, duplicate references, chronology, correction, conservation, separate refunds, aging and snapshot replay; browser tests cover the complete local save/export/import workflow.

## Explicit operating payment plans (13 October)

The optional `operatingPaymentPlan` records explicit future payment dates and integer-halala amounts for existing BUDGET expense items, with separate plan/source and local review references. ACTUAL, NORMALIZED and BENCHMARK records cannot be used as payment-plan items. Annual expense records do not contain a qualified budget period or payment schedule; this feature deliberately does not derive dates, prorate annual costs, certify completeness or reconcile a partial schedule to an assumed year. The UI labels annual amounts as reference values only. Amounts entered in the plan require review against the named plan source.

Projection reports entered payments by calendar month and outside the projection horizon, and identifies budget items with no entered payments. An empty plan means unavailable data, not confirmed zero costs. `coverageComplete` and `cashBalanceCalculated` remain false. A zero month in a populated schedule means no entered payment that month, not independently verified zero spending. Existing normalized OPEX, net income, rent accrual, CAPEX and historical collections are unchanged; there is no double deduction or new net-cash forecast. Past planned dates block projection and new saves instead of becoming actual payments. Advancing the as-of date triggers review. Duplicate item/date entries, nonpositive/unsafe amounts, overflow, mismatched cases and excessive entry counts are rejected. Import replays structural validation; related lease/ledger edits preserve the plan. Draft confirmation resets on edits and stale drafts cannot overwrite changed expenses/plans.

Focused tests cover schedule boundaries, invalid/past dates, strict budget-only selection, missing versus empty data, source/review requirements, sum overflow, non-mutation, date advancement and snapshot replay. Browser acceptance exercises save refusal, confirmation invalidation, successful persistence, no change to normalized net, export/import and isolation between cases.

Still open: compliant invoice/tax credit-note issuance and integrations, refund execution/bank reconciliation/tax/deposits, authenticated institutional ledger and reconciled bank evidence, complete budget-period-qualified operating forecasts and receipt/cash-balance forecasts, automatic lifecycle transitions and scenario-based reletting, specialized asset/tenure valuation, qualified OCR, institutional identity/storage/security/restore, professional/legal release and market/commercial qualification. No private user documents are included in this delivery or CI fixtures.


## Explicit period budget reconciliation (13 October)

A payment plan can now include optional `budgetControl`: an inclusive start, exclusive end and explicit SAR totals for every eligible BUDGET expense item. These are period totals reviewed with the plan's source and review references, never inferred from annual amounts. Every eligible item must appear exactly once, including an explicit zero where supported by the entered source. Invalid dates, missing/duplicate/foreign items, fractional minor units and unsafe totals are rejected.

Reconciliation compares each item's payments within the period to its entered budget in integer halalas; equal grand totals cannot conceal differences between items. Every payment must fall within that period. A past period start or past planned payment requires review. New saves with mismatches are rejected. Structurally valid imported mismatches remain reviewable with item differences, but planned totals/rows are withheld. Advancing the case date rechecks the period. An all-zero explicit period budget can match zero entered payments for the listed inventory, without certifying complete costs or a zero cash balance.

Legacy unbounded plans remain compatible and partial. Turning off period controls requires a newly sourced/reviewed save. Snapshot replay and related edits preserve the optional controls. The UI shows period dates, item budget, payments and remaining/excess differences; confirmation resets on edit, and case switches discard unsaved drafts. NOI, normalized OPEX, CAPEX and existing analytical net are unchanged. This closes explicit period/item arithmetic reconciliation, not independent source verification, complete inventory coverage, actual-payment reconciliation, collection forecasting or cash-balance forecasting.

## 2026-10-14 — explicit receipts and partial cash scenario

Added optional `cashScenario` to the operating-case snapshot, retained during lease and ledger updates. The editor requires an explicit opening balance dated at the start of the case date, distinct source/review references, renewed confirmation, and positive future receipt assumptions linked to supplied leases with unique plan references. Zero receipts require a saved empty plan; an absent plan is unavailable. The opening balance can be explicitly zero or negative. No annual rent, ledger balance or historical receipt is automatically converted to future cash.

Month-end rows subtract current explicit operating and capital payment plans from the entered receipts. Calculation requires the operating period budget to reconcile per supplied item and match the entire projection horizon exactly, and the capital plan to pass existing cost reconciliation. Arithmetic uses integer halalas with BigInt intermediate checks; combined overflow, missing plans, stale dates or invalid horizon withhold all resulting balances. Imported assumptions are recalculated against current plans. Export includes the source case and result. Existing accrual NOI and ledger totals remain separate.

This is **PARTIAL_SCENARIO**, not a complete bank forecast: unentered financing, taxes, deposits, fees and other flows are excluded. Budget matching does not certify inventory completeness. Month-end balances do not establish intramonth liquidity. Source references are local review metadata, not authenticated bank evidence. Physical devices, external integrations, institutional qualification and the intermittent old-entry HTML delivery issue remain open.

Validation: focused regression exercises date boundaries, signed opening balances, explicit zero, missing and mismatched budgets, invalid references, snapshots, lease edits, unchanged historical/accrual data and combined overflow. Browser regression enters and rejects a past receipt, invalidates confirmation, saves a valid scenario, verifies both payment deductions and preserves the scenario across ledger changes and import/export. CI/publication evidence is recorded on the delivery PR and tracker.
