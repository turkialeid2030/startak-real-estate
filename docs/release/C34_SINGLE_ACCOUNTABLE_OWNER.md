## Critical separation of duties

The following concepts are distinct:

1. **Accountable owner** — owns the workstream, ensures the required review/input is obtained, and is responsible for follow-through.
2. **Evidence provider / custodian** — supplies the real underlying artifact/input.
3. **Independent reviewer / authorized approver** — where the governed acceptance boundary requires independence or accountable professional disposition, performs that review or approval.
4. **Governed validator** — the existing C30/C31 software verifies structure, hashes, candidate binding and gate semantics.

One person may be accountable for all 13 workstreams. That does not make that person an independent security reviewer, privacy reviewer, legal counsel, model validator, source custodian, or operational rehearsal witness merely by declaration.
