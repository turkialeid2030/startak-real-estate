# STARTAK — Arabic personal valuation and investment research brief

- Generates a readable Arabic RTL self-contained HTML report from the existing researchReportBundle; the operator may print it to PDF locally.
- Includes explicit HOLD, property city/district and asset type, scenario numbers from the existing deterministic calculator, historical district open-data annex when loaded, source link provenance, per-feed connection coverage, conflicts, risks and no automatic GO.
- HTML output rejects tampered bundle professional approval, certified market value or purchase price. The result remains a personal research report and not a licensed professional appraisal.
- User text is HTML-escaped and evidence hyperlinks require HTTPS, without remote scripts, trackers or third-party resources.
- Missing outside requirements: verified executed sale prices, official title/parcel validation, actual live model APIs, authorized tenant store, professional review and production authority.
