# Dated lease schedules and explicit rent-free periods

The monthly run-off now uses existing STEP_RENT and MANUAL_SCHEDULE contracts rather than rejecting them. It segments each month at dated rent changes, preserving source/adoption references per segment. A scheduled amount replaces periodic rent (in the contract's original frequency) until the next entry or exclusive lease expiry. Zero is explicit rent-free time; no restart is inferred. NONE contracts retain previous arithmetic. Percentage/amount automatic escalation and indexed rent remain unsupported in this projection.

A local editor replaces the complete dated schedule with 1–200 chronological entries inside the lease. It requires source and review references plus confirmation after the last edit. Stale lease drafts are rejected. A case/date change clears the editor. Existing schedule rows survive normal operating-case save/export/import. Shortening a lease across a scheduled change is rejected, not silently truncated. A 10000-change case limit bounds detailed projection work.

The focused test compares segment arithmetic against a separate daily summation across 28 date scenarios and covers explicit zero, resumed rent, missing amounts, duplicate dates, outside-term dates, units, source/adoption links, snapshot round-trip and non-mutation. Three-browser desktop/mobile acceptance exercises the real editor, invalidated confirmation, duplicate-date refusal, successful save, projection export, case export and draft isolation. Final immutable-tree CI and public acceptance are recorded in the PR and #658.

This remains illustrative accrual, not invoicing, bank receipts, regulatory permission or certified valuation. Evidence dates describe information available as of the case; scheduled effective dates may be later. Local review does not authenticate a person or document. There is no automatic main-study mutation, renewal, index forecasting, tax, financing, CAPEX timing or institutional authorization. Source-dependent concessions not entered in the dated schedule remain excluded. FREEHOLD-only scope and other boundaries of the previous run-off remain in force.
